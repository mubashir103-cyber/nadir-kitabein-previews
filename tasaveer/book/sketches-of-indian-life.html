(I) After crawling in this fashion for over half an hour, Jones gets a good chance and fires. (II) He proceeds to search for the dead buck, when a native informs him that the bullet has just slain his grandmother!! (III) After a lengthy palaver, Jones agrees to console the afflicted relatives with the sum of two hundred rupees, and decides to give up blackbuck shooting. (IV) Notwithstanding a gentle hint sent, that he should shoot there again, as old women were plenty and rupees scarce.
